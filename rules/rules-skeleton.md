# Rules Skeleton

This document outlines the current playable structure of Shopkeeper Showdown.

It is not the final rulebook. It exists to define the bare-bones rules needed for the first prototype.

---

## 1. Objective of the Game

Players compete to become the most successful fanatsy shopkeeper.

Over 3 rounds, players send out their adventurer to gather resources, use the town to prepare
and specialise, then complete selling cards to earn coins, reputation, and scoring rewards.

At the end of Round 3, the player with the highest final score is deemed the winner.

---

## 2. Components

Components that will be required are:

* Adventurer Cards (or Board)
* Shop boards
* Adventure Boards
* Town Board
* Selling Cards
* Adventure Cards
* Town Event Cards
* Resource Cards
* Objective Cards
* Coin Tokens
* Reputation Tokens or Tracker
* Dice
* Round Tracker
* First Player Marker
* Adventure Board Markers

Compoenets under consideration are:

* Shop Upgrades

---

## 3. Setup

Each player takes:

* 1 Adventurer Card (or Board)
* 1 Shop Board
* 1 Player Marker
* Choose 4 starting Selling Cards

For Testing:

* Starting Coins
* Starting Resources

Set up the shared play area:

1. Place the Adventure board on the Left of the play area
2. Place the Town Board in the center
3. Shuffle the relevant decks
4. Create the Selling Card market
5. Place the round tracker on Round 1.
6. Decide the first player by rolling.

---

## 4. Round Structure

The game lasts 3 rounds.

Each round has three main phases:

1. Adventure Phase
2. Town Phase
3. Selling Phase

Cleanup happens at the end of each round.

---

## 5. Adventure Phase

Each player takes 5 Adventure turns.

On an Adventure turn, a player may:

1. Move their adventurer.
2. Reveal or interact with a space / use a skill of their Adventurer
3. Resolve a quest, monster, resource, or event.
4. Gain rewards or suffer consequences.
5. Pass play to the next player.

Continue until every player has taken 5 Adventure turns.

---

## 6. Town Phase

Reveal 1 Town Event, and carry it out if required.

Each player takes 5 Town actions.

On a Town action, a player chooses one town location and resolves its effect and then 
moves onto the next player in order.

Possible Town actions include:

* Train their Adventurer
* Craft new Resources
* Upgrade resources
* Visit the Black Market
* Draw or swap Selling Cards
* Convert resources via Alchemy
* Use a Town Professional
* Auction Resources
* Upgrade Shop
* Promote
* Equip Adventurer
* Draw Objective

Continue until every player has taken 5 Town actions.

---

## 7. Selling Phase

Each player may complete as many Selling Cards as they can.

To complete a Selling Card:

1. Choose a Selling Card you can fulfil
2. Spend the required resources or goods.
3. Gain the listed reward
4. Place the completed card into your completed area.

You can also give resources to the Public Selling Card if you can for more coins.

---

## 8. Cleanup

At the end of eahc round:

1. Clear temporary effects.
2. Discard expired cards.
3. Refill markets and draw Selling Cards
4. Refresh town locations.
5. Rotate the first player marker.
6. Advance the round tracker.

After Round 3, move to final scoring.

---

## 9. End of Game

The game ends after the Selling Phase of Round 3.

Players calculate final score from:

* Coins
* Reputation
* Completed Selling Cards
* Shop upgrades
* Adventurer Strength
* Objectives
* Artifacts

The player with the highest score wins.

---

## 10. Glossary

Adventure Phase
The phase where players explore, complete quests, fight monsaters, and gather resources

Town Phase
The phase where players prepare, train, craft, upgrade, and specialise

Selling Card
A card representing a customer, order, contract, commission, or deal.

Shop Board
The player’s personal board used to store resources, upgrades, and completed sales.
